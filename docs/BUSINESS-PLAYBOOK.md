# First-customer playbook: selling listing & lead-management services

Goal: sign the first paying builder or authorised channel partner, with ₹0 marketing spend,
using the site and admin panel you already have. Nothing here is guaranteed: lead volume depends on
the project, its price, location and the market. Never promise a number of leads or sales.

## 1. What you sell

| Package | What the client gets | Proposed price (your decision) |
|---|---|---|
| **Pilot listing** (30 days) | 1 project page with photos, price, RERA details, map; enquiry / brochure / price-list forms; every enquiry shared with the client within 1 working day; weekly report | ₹5,000 – ₹10,000 flat |
| **Listing + lead handling** (monthly) | Up to 3 projects; first call to each enquiry within 2 working hours; follow-up until site visit or "Lost"; weekly report of enquiries, site visits, outcomes | ₹15,000 – ₹25,000 / month |
| **Landing page** (one-off) | A dedicated campaign page for a launch, with UTM-tagged links for the client's own ads | ₹8,000 – ₹15,000 |
| **Success fee** (optional add-on) | A share of brokerage on bookings that came through your enquiries | As per a written channel-partner agreement |

These are **starting points to test**, not market rates. Pick one price per package, quote it the
same way to everyone for the first month, and adjust based on what prospects say.

## 2. Who to approach first

Smaller and mid-size builders, and authorised channel partners, in the cities you already list
(Noida / Greater Noida / YEIDA from the current inventory). Use only business contact details
they publish or give you: site-office visits, their official websites, property expos, referrals.
Do **not** buy or scrape contact lists.

Add each contact in **Admin → Partners** with *Where this contact came from* filled in.

## 3. Outreach workflow (all manual; the panel only tracks it)

1. **Admin → Partners → Add contact.** Status *To contact*, set a follow-up date.
2. Open the contact → **Write a message** → pick *First message*. Personalise the [blanks],
   then *Open in WhatsApp* / *Open in email app* and send it yourself. The history records that a
   template was opened, not that it was delivered.
3. Set status *Contacted*. Set a follow-up date 3 days out.
4. One polite follow-up at most (*Polite follow-up* template). No reply → *Lost*. "STOP" or
   "not interested" → tick *Do not contact*.
5. Reply → *Replied* → book a 15-minute call → *Meeting*.
6. Meeting: run the demo (section 5). Send the *After the meeting* summary → *Proposal*.
7. Signed and paid → *Won*. Then create their listing (section 6).

## 4. Weekly targets (for one person, part-time)

| Activity | Target / week | Where it's measured |
|---|---|---|
| New contacts added (with source) | 15 | Partners → All |
| First messages sent | 15 | Weekly report → *Messages prepared* |
| Follow-ups done on time | 100% of due | Partners → *Follow-up due* = 0 by Friday |
| Conversations (replied) | 3 | Weekly report → *Moved to Replied* |
| Meetings / demos | 1–2 | *Moved to Meeting* |
| Proposals | 1 | *Moved to Proposal* |
| Paid pilots | 1 within the first 4–6 weeks | *Moved to Won* |
| Own enquiries called within 2 h | 100% | Leads → *Overdue* stays at 0 |

Review the **Weekly report** every Monday. If replies are below 2 per 15 messages after two
weeks, change the message or the audience, not the volume.

## 5. Demo with honest data

- **Best:** show your real admin panel with real listings and real enquiry counts. Hide lead names
  and numbers (the client must not see other people's buyers).
- **If you have few enquiries:** create a clearly labelled demo listing as a **Draft** (never
  published, title starting "DEMO —"), and walk through the enquiry → lead → follow-up → report
  flow with your own phone number. Delete the demo lead afterwards (mark *Lost*).
- Say plainly how many enquiries the site currently gets. Don't show fabricated numbers.

## 6. Before publishing a client's project

Collect and keep on file:

- RERA registration number and certificate. Check it on the state RERA portal (UP RERA for
  Noida / Greater Noida / YEIDA) and record the check in *Source & verification*.
- Written authorisation to market the project: builder letter, or channel-partner agreement
  naming you.
- The approved price list and brochure, with dates.
- Photos and renders you have the right to use (supplied by the builder; no images taken from
  other portals).

Then add the listing, fill *Where these details came from*, set *Verified* only after checking,
and look at **Listing quality** before making it live.

## 7. Compliance checklist (India) — not legal advice

- **RERA (Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016).** Registered projects must be
  advertised with their RERA registration number and the authority's website. If you advertise
  or facilitate sales, check whether you must register as a real estate agent with the state
  authority (UP RERA agent registration for UP projects) and show your agent number. Don't
  advertise unregistered projects that require registration.
- **Advertising.** No "assured returns", "guaranteed appreciation" or similar unless documented
  and permitted. The Listing quality page flags these words. Prices are "starting from" and
  must match the builder's current price list. The ASCI code applies to ads.
- **Privacy (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).** Use enquiry details only to respond to
  that enquiry. Share a lead with a builder only for the project the person asked about. Honour
  "stop" requests (Do not contact). Delete on request. Keep the retention script running.
- **Messaging.** WhatsApp Business policy forbids unsolicited bulk messages. Send one-to-one,
  relevant messages only. Promotional SMS / calls are covered by TRAI's TCCCPR rules (DND). Don't
  cold-call buyers who haven't enquired.
- **Contracts.** Put every paid engagement in writing: scope, fee, duration, who owns the leads,
  how leads are shared, confidentiality, data protection, termination, and the builder's
  warranty that listing details, images and RERA information are accurate and authorised.
- **Tax.** Invoice properly. GST registration is required above the turnover threshold or for
  some inter-state services. Ask a CA.
